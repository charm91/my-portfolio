# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-08-01

## Who We Are

This site (nguwahaung.com) is the personal portfolio of Ngu Wah Aung ("Charm"), a Senior Product Designer. It showcases product design case studies and provides a way for visitors to get in touch. This policy explains what personal data the site collects and how it is handled.

## What Data We Collect

- **Contact form submissions.** When you use the contact form, we collect your name, email address, and the content of your message.
- **Standard technical data.** Like any website, our hosting infrastructure automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and request logs as part of delivering the site to you. This data is not used to identify you personally.

We do not collect account information, payment details, or any special category data (such as health, biometric, or genetic data).

## How We Use Your Data

- **To respond to your enquiry.** We use the name, email, and message you submit through the contact form to reply to you. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), given by voluntarily submitting the form.
- **To operate and secure the site.** Technical/server log data is processed to deliver the site reliably and protect it against abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in operating a functioning, secure website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

## Data Sharing and Third Parties

- **EmailJS** — the contact form is delivered using EmailJS, a third-party email service, which processes your name, email, and message solely to route them to us.
- **Vercel** — the site is hosted on Vercel, which processes standard technical/server log data as part of serving the site.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with any third party for marketing purposes.

## International Data Transfers

Our third-party service providers (EmailJS and Vercel) may process data outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where this happens, we rely on the safeguards those providers have in place, such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent measures, to protect your data.

## Data Retention

Contact form messages are kept only as long as needed to respond to your enquiry and for a reasonable follow-up period, after which they are deleted. Technical/server logs are retained by our hosting provider for a limited period for security and operational purposes, per their own retention practices.

## Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request erasure of your data
- Request restriction of how we process your data
- Receive your data in a portable format
- Object to our processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, use the contact details below.

## Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

## Supervisory Authority

If you believe our processing of your data violates applicable law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
